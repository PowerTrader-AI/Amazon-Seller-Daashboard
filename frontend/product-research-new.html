How to find products YOU can win

The top 5 bestsellers have 10,000–30,000 reviews — they are untouchable for a new seller.
But ranks #20–#100 often have products with 50–500 reviews, good demand, and no Amazon — these are your opportunities.
Strategy: Find products with BSR < 30,000 (real demand) + reviews < 500 (low barrier) + Amazon not selling = winnable gap.

Lower = easier to rank against

ReviewsHow many customer reviews top sellers have. <200 = easy to compete. 200–1000 = manageable. >1000 = hard to rank.

Price + TrendCurrent selling price. ▲ = rising (good margin). ▼ = dropping (price war). ₹400–₹2000 is the India sweet spot.

Est. Sales/monthRough estimate from BSR. Not exact but useful for comparing demand. A product with 150 sales/month at ₹800 = ₹1.2L revenue pool.

Can I Win?WIN = great opportunity. MAYBE = worth analysing. WATCH = Amazon competing but high demand. HARD = too competitive.
